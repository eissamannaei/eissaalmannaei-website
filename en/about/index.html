Story

Eissa Almannaei is a UAE-based AI Researcher, Speaker, and Agentic AI Specialist. He works to make artificial intelligence practical, understandable, and genuinely useful for institutions, youth, and knowledge-driven organizations.

His path moved from engineering and technical program leadership into artificial intelligence — where he now focuses on turning frontier capability into applied, dependable systems rather than abstract promise.

He works across AI capacity building, agentic AI, responsible AI, research automation, and institutional readiness. Through talks, workshops, and working prototypes, he helps teams move from simply using AI tools to building real capabilities with them.

His core specialty is agentic AI: agents that don't just answer prompts but carry out multi-step tasks, monitor, and act. He builds and studies these systems and helps organizations understand where they fit — and where they don't.

His aim is simple: turn AI from something people consume into a capability that institutions and communities can build, govern, and trust.
